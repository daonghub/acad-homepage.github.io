## About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by [Shuoqing Deng](https://sites.google.com/view/shuoqingdeng/homepage) and [Yang Xiang](https://www.math.hkust.edu.hk/~maxiang/).

Before joining HKUST, I completed an MSc in Statistical Finance at Imperial College London in 2023. I graduated from Central South University with a degree in Statistics in 2022.

## Research interests

My current research focuses on graphon particle systems/Mean field games.
