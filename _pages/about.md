## About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by DENG Shuoqing and XIANG Yang.

Before joining HKUST, I completed an MSc in Statistical Finance at Imperial College London in 2023. I graduated from Central South University with a degree in Statistics in 2022.

## Research Interests

My current research focuses on graphon particle systems and mean field games.
